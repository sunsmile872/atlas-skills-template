# EDX / Neuromuscular Mode Blueprint (Flexible A4 Pages)

Use this structure for MND/ALS, CIDP, GBS, Myasthenia Gravis, Plexopathy, Radiculopathy, Neuropathy, and Myopathy Grand Rounds. Keep sections in this clinical order and let content span as many clean A4 pages as needed without cramping.

## Modular Section Architecture

1. **Page 1 — Pathophysiology, Epidemiology & Clinical Course**
   - Selective anatomic/cellular targets vs. spared structures.
   - Genetics/immunology, incidence/prevalence, prognosis.
   - Onset phenotypes with frequencies, contiguous/temporal spread pattern, and associated systemic/cognitive features.
2. **Page 2 — Deep Variant Correlation & Management**
   - Table mapping every major **Variant / Mimic** → Pathology → Clinical Course → **Expected Bedside Exam Findings**.
   - Current Disease-Modifying Therapies (verify FDA approvals/withdrawals) + PM&R care (PFT/NIV thresholds, dysphagia/PEG timing, exercise prescription, symptomatic meds).
3. **Page 3 — Stage 1 & 2A: Clinical History Approach + Dual-System Neuro Exam (General, CN & Upper Limb)**
   - 4-Stage SVG workflow header (`History & Exam → Criteria Check → Discuss & EDX Plan → Interpretation`).
   - History split into: **A. Tempo, Spread & Motor/Sensory Clues** vs. **B. Exclusionary Screening & Etiologic Context**.
   - Dual-System Exam Part 1 (`System` | `Key Maneuvers & How-to Key Traps` | `Expected Findings` | `Clinical Why / DDx Link`), including *Tongue resting inside mouth*, *Jaw jerk*, *FDP vs. FDS*, *Brachioradialis*, *Hoffmann/Tromner*, *Inverted radial reflex*, and Frontera's *"2+ reflex in wasted limb"* pearl.
4. **Page 4 — Stage 2B: Dual-System Neuro Exam (Thoracic, Lower Limb, Mimic Traps) + Region Summary Map**
   - Cover Thoracic/Bedside Resp (`Paradoxical abdominal breathing` supine, `Single-breath count`, `Superficial abdominal skin reflex`), Lower Limb (`TA vs TP/Gastroc`, `Quadriceps + Patellar`, `Hip flexors/abductors`, `Crossed adductor`, `Babinski/Clonus`), Sensory/Posterior column (`Vibration + Romberg`), Cerebellar, Spine provocative + Bedside mimic traps (`Grip/Percussion myotonia`, `Sustained upgaze`, `Gynecomastia`), and Gait/PM&R function.
   - **Summary Map Table:** Translate system exam findings directly into the disease's diagnostic regions/compartments (e.g., Craniobulbar / Cervical / Thoracic / Lumbosacral UMN vs. LMN).
5. **Section 5 — Diagnostic Criteria Mastery (Apply Exam Map to Criteria)**
   - **Anatomical Rationale ("Why"):** Explicitly explain why regions/compartments are divided that way and why minimum muscle/nerve counts differ across regions (e.g., excluding focal entrapment or single radiculopathy).
   - **Define on First Mention:** Define composite EMG/clinical terms (e.g., *Chronic* vs. *Active*, *Weakness + Wasting*) immediately where introduced, and retain full qualifiers inside comparison table cells.
   - **Prerequisites & Regional/Muscle Count Rules** (including UMN = clinical only; LMN = clinical or EMG + exact minimum counts).
   - **3-Era Comparison Table** comparing prior vs. current diagnostic criteria (e.g., rEEC vs. Awaji vs. Gold Coast 2020; or Brighton vs. EAN/PNS 2021) including sensitivity/specificity trade-offs.
   - **4–5 Worked Scenario Table** showing how borderline/trap cases classify under prior vs. current criteria.
   - **EMG Scoring Rules & Non-Qualifying Traps:** Explicit "2-leg" rule alongside common pitfalls that do NOT qualify alone.
6. **Page 6 — Stage 3A: Pre-EDX Clinical Discussion & DDx-to-EDX Planning Matrix**
   - **Step A:** Anatomical Localization logic.
   - **Step B:** Phenotype-Driven DDx Grouping.
   - **DDx-to-EDX Planning Matrix:** 1:1 mapping from each DDx → Hypothesis → Specific NCS/EMG tests ordered → Discriminating findings.
   - Concise spoken transition statement bridging Discussion into EDX Planning.
7. **Page 7 — Stage 3B: EDX Execution Protocol (NCS & Needle EMG)**
   - Routine Motor & Sensory NCS + specific ratios (e.g., Split-Hand `APB/ADM < 0.6`, `FDI/ADM < 0.9`) + Late responses + Optional RNS/SFEMG.
   - Explicit demyelination & Conduction Block criteria (`CV < 75% LLN`, `DL > 130% ULN`, non-entrapment area drop `> 50%`, proximal stimulation cautions).
   - Needle EMG muscle selection rules (distal + proximal, different nerves, different roots across ≥ 3 limbs; thoracic paraspinals avoiding T11–T12; bulbar pitfalls) + Bedside fasciculation/NMUS technique.
8. **Page 8 — Stage 4A: Expected EDX Findings (Parameter-by-Parameter) & Systematic Interpretation**
   - Parameter-by-parameter table covering expected SNAP, CMAP/DL/CV, Late responses, RNS/SFEMG, Needle EMG (Insertional, Spontaneous, MUAP morphology, Recruitment & Activation), physiological rationale, and allowable thresholds.
   - 4-step reading order + formal EDX impression template (and {{INSTITUTION}} report format when analyzing a real case).
9. **Page 9 — Stage 4B: Diagnostic Pitfalls, Top 5 Mimics & Post-EDX Workup**
   - Common EDX Pitfalls (e.g., slow RNS decrement from immature collateral sprouting, anti-MuSK tongue fasciculations, Recruitment vs. Activation, preganglionic SNAP sparing, myotonic discharges vs. fibrillations).
   - Top 5 Mimics Differential Matrix + Post-EDX Imaging/Labs/PM&R Baseline checklist.
