# Neurorehab / PM&R Mode Blueprint (Flexible A4 Pages)

Use this structure for **Stroke, Spinal Cord Injury (SCI), Traumatic Brain Injury (TBI)**, and comprehensive rehabilitation Grand Rounds. Let content span as many clean A4 pages as needed without cramping. (For **Hybrid cases** such as post-stroke brachial plexopathy or SCI conus vs. cauda equina, merge the EDX planning & interpretation modules with Rehab.)

## Modular Section Architecture

1. **Page 1 — Pathophysiology, Vascular/Anatomic Territory & Prognosis**
   - **Stroke:** Ischemic (MCA/ACA/PCA/Brainstem/Lacunar) vs. Hemorrhagic; penumbra vs. core; neuroplasticity & golden period timeline.
   - **SCI:** Primary vs. secondary injury; spinal shock vs. neurogenic shock; complete vs. incomplete prognosis & neural recovery curve.
   - Associated systemic/cognitive impairments (e.g., aphasia, neglect, depression, FTD/executive, autonomic failure).
2. **Page 2 — Syndromes, Classifications & Medical/Pharmacologic Management**
   - **Stroke:** Territory syndromes table (MCA superior/inferior, ACA, PCA, Wallenberg, Weber, Millard-Gubler, Lacunar) + expected exam findings.
   - **SCI:** Incomplete syndromes (Central cord, Brown-Séquard, Anterior cord, Posterior cord, Conus medullaris vs. Cauda equina) + expected exam findings.
   - Acute/secondary prevention & neurorehab pharmacology (antiplatelets/anticoagulants, spasticity meds, neuropathic pain, bone/DVT prophylaxis).
3. **Page 3 — Stage 1: Rehab History Approach & Functional Staging Criteria**
   - 4-Stage SVG workflow header (`History → Dual-System & Functional Exam → Problem-List Discussion → Targeted Rehab Program`).
   - History clusters: Onset/interventions (tPA/MT, decompression/fixation, weight-bearing precautions), **Pre-morbid function & home environment** (stairs, bathroom, caregiver), Swallowing/airway, Bladder/bowel habits, Cardiovascular/hemodynamic limits.
   - Staging/Classification table with **Anatomical/Functional Why** and **Define on First Mention**: **NIHSS / Brunnstrom Stages (1–6) / MAS** (Stroke) or **ISNCSCI / ASIA Impairment Scale (AIS A–E)** (SCI — explaining why S4–S5 sacral sparing separates Complete vs. Incomplete, and how ZPP/Bulbocavernosus work).
4. **Page 4 — Stage 2A: Dual-System Neuro Exam (General, CN, Perception/Speech & Upper Limb)**
   - Columns: `System` | `Key Maneuvers & How-to Key Traps` | `Expected Findings` | `Clinical Why / Rehab Impact`.
   - Include `Key-Traps`:
     - *Neglect / Extinction:* Line bisection, clock drawing, double simultaneous stimulation (differentiate hemianopia vs. neglect).
     - *Pusher Syndrome:* Observe upright sitting/transfer posture and resistance when correcting tilt toward non-paretic side.
     - *Aphasia:* Fluency, comprehension, repetition, naming (Broca vs. Wernicke vs. Transcortical vs. Global).
     - *Brunnstrom Synergy vs. Isolated Movement* & *Hemiplegic Shoulder* (inferior subluxation sulcus, external rotation ROM, impingement pain).
     - *SCI Key Muscles C5–T1* & *Tenodesis grasp* assessment (C6 wrist extension).
5. **Page 5 — Stage 2B: Dual-System Exam (Trunk, Lower Limb, Sensory, Bedside Screens) + Classification Summary Map**
   - *Trunk & Balance:* Sitting balance (static/dynamic), standing balance, Respiratory/cough (especially high cervical/thoracic SCI).
   - *Lower Limb:* Key muscles L2–S1, Spasticity (MAS) vs. Clonus, Babinski.
   - *SCI ISNCSCI Key-Traps:* **28 Key Dermatomes** (light touch + pinprick), **Sacral Sparing (S4–S5 LT/PP + Voluntary Anal Contraction [VAC] + Deep Anal Pressure [DAP])** to separate Complete vs. Incomplete, **Bulbocavernosus reflex** (end of spinal shock), **Zone of Partial Preservation (ZPP)**.
   - *Dysphagia Screen:* **MWST (Modified Water Swallow Test)**, gag/cough, wet voice, oral phase residue.
   - **Summary Map Table:** Map system findings cleanly into **Neurological Level + AIS Grade** (SCI) or **Territory + Brunnstrom Stage + Impairment List** (Stroke).
6. **Page 6 — Stage 3: Pre-Rehab Clinical Discussion & Problem-to-Intervention Matrix**
   - **Step A:** Lesion & Prognostic Synthesis (golden period potential, cardiovascular/orthopedic precautions).
   - **Step B:** Problem-to-Intervention Matrix linking every impairment directly to diagnostic tests & rehab interventions:
     - *Dysphagia* → NPO/IDDSI level, indirect vs. direct swallowing exercises, FEES/VFSS & PAS scale.
     - *Neurogenic Bladder* → Suprapontine (uninhibited/timed voiding/PVR) vs. Suprasacral (DSD → CIC + anticholinergic/beta-3, DLPP < 40 cmH₂O) vs. Sacral/LMN (flaccid → CIC, avoid Crede if reflux risk).
     - *Motor/Synergy/Gait* → Brunnstrom-specific PT/OT facilitation, AFO/resting splint, hemiplegic shoulder protection.
     - *Cognitive/Neglect/Pusher* → Visual scanning, vertical cues, caregiver education.
7. **Page 7 — Complications Watchlist & Emergency Protocols**
   - **SCI:** **Autonomic Dysreflexia (AD)** (lesions ≥ T6: BP ≥ 20–40 mmHg rise, pounding headache, sweating above level → sit up, loosen tight clothing, check Foley/bowel impaction first), Orthostatic hypotension, Heterotopic Ossification (HO), DVT/PE, Pressure injury stages & turning schedule, Neurogenic bowel program.
   - **Stroke:** Aspiration pneumonia, Shoulder-hand syndrome (CRPS), DVT, Falls, Post-stroke depression/seizure, Exercise hemodynamic limits (e.g., active IE / recent stent / uncontrolled HTN).
8. **Page 8 — Functional Goals, Assistive Technology & Discharge Planning**
   - Expected functional independence milestones (by C4/C5/C6/C7/T1/Paraplegia level in SCI; or Ambulation/ADL prognosis in Stroke).
   - Orthotics, Wheelchair & Adaptive Equipment prescription (Tilt-in-space, cushion, AFO, Tenodesis splint, communication boards).
   - Multidisciplinary OPD follow-up & caregiver training checklist.
