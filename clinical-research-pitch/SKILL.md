# Clinical Research Pitch & Feasibility Scout

Phase 1–2 front-end workflow for Thai medical and residency research across any specialty. Bridges raw literature tools (`pubmed-database`, `literature-search-openalex`, `clinical-trials-database`) and IRB drafting (`thai-hospital-irb-grant-builder`).

## Core Guardrails
1. **Zero-Fabrication & Full Citation Labels**: Verify every PMID, DOI, article title, author, sample size (`N`), correlation (`r`), and p-value from live tool output or full-text PDFs. Never guess numbers or cite memory. **Never cite bare PMIDs/DOIs alone and never merge multiple distinct papers into a single table row**—give EVERY cited study its own dedicated row with **Author et al., Journal (Year), Study Title, PMID/PMCID, sample/arms (`N`), and exact quantitative results (`p`-values, CIs, SDs, `%` change)** so advisors see every study in full without looking up IDs.
2. **Small-N & Effort First**: Never default to a high-burden `N >= 140` prospective Prevalence design without explicitly comparing lower-effort, smaller-`N` alternatives (`Immediate Crossover N = 20–35`, `Correlation / ROC-AUC N = 35–60`, `FPC Survey`, or `0-visit Retrospective Chart Review`).
3. **Copyright & Git Hygiene**: Never bypass gated tool registration (e.g., EuroQol `EQ-5D-5L`). Extract only essential pages from bulky open CPG PDFs and run visual render verification. Keep generated `.pdf` files local (gitignored via `projects/**/*.pdf`); copy to iCloud Drive only when explicitly requested.
4. **Thai Medical Academic Register & Anti-Calque Gate (`academic-humanizer` Layer 7)**: Write Pitch Notes in authentic Thai medical resident/faculty voice—define the Thai term with its English medical term once, then use natural code-switching/loanwords (`double-blind`, `blinded assessor`, `carryover effect`, `ค่า PPT [ระดับความไวต่อแรงกดเจ็บ]`, `ค่า SWV`, `จุดกดเจ็บ active MTrP`, `ค่า Bang's blinding index`, `คะแนน NDI`). **NEVER use literal AI dictionary calques** (`ธรณีความเจ็บปวด`, `ปกปิดสองทาง`, `สุ่มขนาน`, `ผู้ประเมินปกปิด`, `ผลค้างข้ามรอบ`, `จุดกดเจ็บที่ทำงานอยู่`, `ความบกพร่องของคอ`, `ท่ายืดเกร็ง`, `เชิงอัตวิสัย`, `พุทธิปัญญา`, `ได้พิสูจน์แล้วว่า`, `ปริศนาจากงาน`, `ตอบคำถามที่ค้างคา` — blocked by `deliverable_auditor.py`).

## Stage 1 — Advisor & Gap Scouting (Gate Before Generating PDFs)
1. **4-Source Deep Literature & Advisor Sweep (Mandatory Standard)**:
   Run `scripts/advisor_portfolio_scan.py` (with `--abstracts`, `--pubmed-meta`, `--openalex-works`, `--epmc-query`, `--ct-term`) **PLUS** `search_web` targeting **Google Scholar / ThaiJO / TCI** before proposing or locking any topic:
   - **PubMed Primary Trials & Advisor Portfolio (`--abstracts`)**: Pull full abstracts of ALL advisor studies in the domain (to capture exact washout/sham/scale conventions) and recent primary trials (including **negative/null sham-controlled trials** so known dead-ends are avoided).
   - **PubMed Meta-Analyses & GRADE Reviews (`--pubmed-meta`)**: Always check the latest 2–5 year Systematic Reviews/Meta-Analyses to identify certainty downgrades (e.g., lack of sham control, unblinded assessors, high heterogeneity).
   - **OpenAlex + Europe PMC + ClinicalTrials.gov**: Catch regional/Asian/non-PubMed journals and ongoing registered protocols.
   - **Google Scholar / ThaiJO / TCI (`search_web`)**: Check Thai university journals and local theses so no local thesis/ThaiJO publication is overlooked or duplicated.
2. **Propose 3–5 Ranked Candidates**:
   Present 3–5 non-duplicative options tailored to the resident's feasible modalities/clinic access, ranked by **real-world patient burden** and **required `N`** verified with `scripts/sample_size_calc.py`. Explicitly warn about any negative sham-controlled precedent or GRADE certainty trap found in Step 1.
3. **Lock 1 Candidate**:
   Wait for the user to confirm 1 winning direction and study design before generating Stage 2 deliverables.

## Stage 2 — Stress-Test, Pitch Note PDF & Instrument Pack
1. **Full-Text Anchor Audit**:
   Read available full-text PDFs of the advisor's key anchor paper and the latest benchmark paper. Extract exact `N`, inclusion/exclusion criteria, scoring directionality (watch for inverted scales), and explicit limitations/future-study calls.
2. **Mock Reviewer Gap Stress-Test**:
   - Challenge whether the research question survives tough peer review ("known phenomenon?" -> sharpen with a **Clinical Mismatch**, **Health Utility / QALY**, **Setting-Paradox**, **Assessor-Blinded Sham Control**, or **Objective Physical Primary Outcome** [e.g., Ultrasound `2D-SWE` stiffness `m/s` instead of already-answered subjective/psychophysical endpoints like `PPT` or `VAS` alone] addressing recent Meta-Analysis GRADE downgrades and avoiding documented sham-negative interventions).
   - Synthesize an explicit **60-Second Plain-Thai 3-Step Storytelling Funnel** on Page 1 (*Step 1: What prior studies/local team already proved—explicitly naming which intervention vs. comparator and which exact outcome was measured -> Step 2: The exact unanswered clinical/methodological gaps and why subjective/psychophysical endpoints alone are insufficient -> Step 3: How our design solves all prior flaws*). Move dense quantitative metrics, CIs, and PMIDs to the **Page 3 One-Study-Per-Row Evidence Table** so Page 1 reads effortlessly in 60 seconds.
   - Include a dedicated **Advisor Lineage & Extension Analysis** section mapping: (1) what the advisor/department already proved in prior papers, (2) exact limits/unanswered domains in their prior studies (e.g., underpowered `N`, crossover carryover risk, self-reported outcome bias), and (3) concrete upgrades/extensions our study delivers.
   - Distill the study into a **1-Sentence Core Story + Structured Research Questions (`1 Primary` tied directly to the sample-size calculation + `1–2 Secondary/Exploratory` questions) + Explicit Two-Sided Statistical Hypotheses ($H_0: \mu_A = \mu_B$ vs. $H_1: \mu_A \neq \mu_B$ matching two-sided $\alpha = 0.05$)** on Page 1 so PICO, Outcomes, Hypotheses, and `N` map 1:1 without ambiguity.
   - **Carryover vs. Parallel ANCOVA Decision Rule**: For interventions with physiological or analgesic carryover lasting $>24\text{ hr}$ (e.g., `rPMS`, `WBV`, exercise), warn about Crossover Carryover (`Effect + Carryover` confounding Period 2 baseline) and recommend a **1-Visit Double-Blind Parallel Sham-RCT analyzed with Baseline-Adjusted ANCOVA (`Borm factor 1 - ρ²`)** as the primary design, showing Crossover (`even N`) as a secondary reference row. For purely mechanical/immediate-washout modalities (e.g., elastic taping/orthosis), verify **even `N` (`--crossover`)** or **Williams Latin Square (`multiple of 2k`)** with baseline re-check before each period.
   - Draft **IRB-Ready Operational Inclusion/Exclusion Criteria** with explicit clinical thresholds (baseline severity floor to avoid ceiling/floor effects on objective imaging, recent BoNT-A/injection/analgesic washout, BMI/acoustic window limits, no proxy self-report on subjective outcomes) and lock a **Strict Measurement Sequence** (e.g., `1. VAS -> 2. Non-contact/low-pressure Ultrasound 2D-SWE -> 3. Algometer PPT -> 4. Full-range ROM` so mechanical compression or stretching never alters tissue properties before imaging).
   - Split clinical variables into **Must-Have** (collected in 5–6 min or reliably in EMR) vs. **Optional** (skip if missing in OPD charts so recruitment never stalls), and note **Trial Registration (`TCTR`) + Blinded Assessor logistics** whenever designing an interventional/crossover trial. For **Crossover designs**, verify **even `N` (`--crossover`)** for `1:1 AB/BA` allocation balance and explicitly define a **Washout / Carryover plan** (e.g., baseline re-check before Period 2 if same-day washout) + **Blinding integrity check**.
   - Draft **4 concrete Clinical Translation & Utilization impacts** (clinical screening, local context, health-economic/policy utility, and future RCT stepping stone).
3. **Build Pitch Note & CRF (Markdown-First -> A4 PDF)**:
   Load [`references/pitch_and_crf_templates.md`](references/pitch_and_crf_templates.md) for the Navy/Teal/Mint HTML templates. Save `.md` sources (`01_Advisor_Pitch_Note.md` or `01A_...` / `01B_...` when keeping side-by-side advisor options, plus `instruments/CRF_*.md`), populate **clean Pure HTML** (`<em>`, `<strong>`, `&rarr;`, `<sup>/<sub>` and HTML entities—**ZERO inline gray `<code>` pill boxes** inside Thai prose or table cells, keeping `<code>` out so sentences read smoothly; move exhaustive regression equations/NCT lists to `SOT` or `Protocol`), and render A4 PDFs via `scripts/render_pitch_pdf.py` (**2-page Quick Pitch** default, or **strictly 3-page Executive Visual Dossier** when full Literature Review + 4-Gap + Advisor Lineage + 10-study Table + 5 Decisions are needed). Visually inspect every rendered PNG page before delivery.
4. **Package Instruments**:
   Download open-access Thai questionnaires or extract essential pages from Thai public CPG PDFs into `instruments/` (and verify render). For copyrighted scales (e.g., EuroQol `EQ-5D-5L`), provide exact free academic registration instructions in `instruments/README_Instruments_and_Links.md`.
5. **Mandatory Post-Pitch Prompt — Optional Resident Research Defense Playbook**:
   Immediately after delivering the Advisor Pitch Note PDF, **ALWAYS explicitly ask the user** whether they would also like a **Resident Research Defense Playbook (`04_Resident_Research_Defense_Playbook_[Topic].md` + Formal Monochrome OMML `.docx`)** tailored for first-time researchers preparing to defend their topic to their advisor and department committee.
   If the user accepts, generate a 7-chapter first-principles playbook (audited by Gate 11 Citation/Factual Parity + Gate 12 Technical/OMML & Layer 7 Thai Register) containing:
   - **Chapter 1 (Clinician-Authentic 3-Step PubMed & Trial Registry Check):** Provide **3 concise, realistic free-text/plain-Boolean PubMed queries** (`P × I` $\rightarrow$ `P × I × O` gap proof $\rightarrow$ `P × O` benchmark) verified live via `pubmed_api.py` (exact hit counts + top PMIDs matching the Pitch Note/Protocol references 1:1), a brief check of `TCTR` / `ClinicalTrials.gov` / `ThaiJO`, and a short natural spoken Thai search script. **HARD RULES:** (a) **Never reverse-engineer `[Author]` tags or inject off-topic conditions** (e.g., `OR "spasticity"` inside an MPS query) just to pull in an advisor's prior paper—separate non-target-disease advisor papers into a **Methodological/Technical Precedent** subsection; (b) **Never put PRISMA-style Screening Ledgers (`Identified -> Excluded N`, `CT-1..4`) or bloated 10-synonym `[Mesh]` blocks** into the Playbook or Pitch Note (keep exhaustive systematic search logs strictly inside `SOT.md` as backend provenance).
   - **Chapter 2 (Paper-by-Paper Literature Lineage):** Detailed breakdown of the advisor's/department's anchor studies (verifying exact hospital study sites & IRB numbers from full text) and key global/contradictory studies, quoting their exact Limitations and showing how our design resolves each flaw.
   - **Chapter 3 (Why These Primary & Secondary Research Questions):** Intuitive plain-Thai explanation of the central scientific mystery, why the Primary Endpoint and each Secondary Endpoint exist, and why the study forms a **Win-Win Design**.
   - **Chapter 4 (Methodology & Design Rationale):** Plain-language defense of study design (e.g., Parallel vs. Crossover, 1-Visit logistics, Sham mechanics, strict measurement order, tear-off blinding, and `Bang's Blinding Index`).
   - **Chapter 5 (Step-by-Step Sample Size Derivation):** Line-by-line plain-Thai math from anchor study means/SDs/cutoffs $\rightarrow$ unadjusted `t-test` $\rightarrow$ `Borm ANCOVA` ($1 - \rho^2$) $\rightarrow$ QC/dropout buffer & exact power, plus a 45-second spoken script.
   - **Chapter 6 (Statistical Comparison Map & 4-Scenario Outcome Matrix):** What compares with what (`Table 1–4`), why each test is chosen, and how to interpret and publish under every possible outcome pattern.
   - **Chapter 7 (4-Dimension Clinical/Academic Impact + 10 Ready-to-Speak Advisor Q&A Scripts):** Authentic {{INSTITUTION}}/Thai Morning Conference spoken answers aligned 1:1 with the Protocol, PIS/ICF, and Budget.
6. **Handoff**:
   Lock Topic, PICO, `N`, and verified PMIDs into `SOT.md` (and `ROADMAP.md` / `projects-summary.md` if in an Atlas project). Once the advisor approves the pitch, hand off to `thai-hospital-irb-grant-builder` (Phase 3 IRB/Proposal) and `clinical-academic-editor` (Phase 3–4 audit).
